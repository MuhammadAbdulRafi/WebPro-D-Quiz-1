Padang, West Sumatra

My hometown is Padang, the capital and largest city of the Indonesian province of West Sumatra. It is beautifully situated on the western coast of Sumatra, facing the Indian Ocean, and backed by the Barisan Mountains.

Padang is renowned as the heartland of the Minangkabau people. The city is famous for its rich culture, distinct traditional architecture known as Rumah Gadang (characterized by roof architectures resembling buffalo horns), and its global culinary influence. It is a city that perfectly balances coastal trade history with deep-rooted cultural traditions.
